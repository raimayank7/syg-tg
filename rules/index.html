Directory rules

What groups and channels may be listed here: sensible, original, lawful content. What is refused: adult material, piracy, betting, hacking and get-rich schemes.

We list groups and channels that are useful, lawful and safe for a general audience. Our rules are simple.

We list

  • Study, work, hobby, local and community groups.
  • News and information channels that name their sources.
  • Original or licensed entertainment.

We refuse

  • Adult or sexual content, including "chat" and "friendship" groups that are really that.
  • Piracy: pirated films, paid courses, books or software, and leaked material.
  • Betting, gambling, satta and fixed-match tips.
  • Hacking, cracking and carding.
  • Get-rich-quick, double-your-money and loan-app schemes.
  • Hate against any person or group.
  • Anything that targets children.

How it is enforced

Blocked words are refused when you submit. Anyone can report a listing; three reports hide it, and two if the reason is adult, scam or piracy. We do not review every listing by hand, so please report what you see.

Your listing

Use a plain name and description with no links and no @names. Submit a listing.
